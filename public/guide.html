1. Platform overview

The Contact Group platform is a shared, multi-user system for managing prospects and the sales pipeline across all group companies (Factoring, Leasing, Mortgage, Credit, Insurance, Contact Auto). Every user signs in with their own account; data is shared in real time, so when one person adds a prospect the relevant people are notified and can act on it.

The platform centres on three things:

  • Prospects & pipeline — capture a prospect, route it through Head of Products validation, then move it through the negotiation stages to a closed deal (which becomes a merchant in the Master Ledger).
  • Governance — the Head of Products validates every new entry; extensions, done deals, edits and deletions all require approval; the Administrator is notified of every submitted request.
  • Controlled reference data — prospect names come from the Egyptian companies directory, and the industry / product lists are centrally managed so every dropdown stays consistent.

3. Getting started — login & registration

Open the platform URL. You can either sign in with an existing account or register a new one.

Sign in

  1. On the Sign In tab, enter your username (the part of your work email before @ — e.g. john.saad) and password.
  2. Click Sign In. You land on your role-specific dashboard.

Register a new account

  1. Click the Register tab.
  2. Enter full name, a username (min 3 characters), email, department, job title.
  3. Set a password — minimum 8 characters, one uppercase letter, one number — and confirm it.
  4. Click Create Account; you are signed in immediately.

5. Feature guide (screen by screen)

Dashboard

Role-aware home screen. Employees/RMs see their own book and pipeline; the Head of Products sees items awaiting validation/approval; CEO/MD/Admin see group-wide figures. A + New pipeline entry button is always available.

Notifications (bell / inbox)

Email-style alerts addressed to you. New-prospect alerts go to the whole leadership list; validation requests go to the Head of Products; approvals/returns go back to the entrant; the Administrator receives an alert for every submitted request. The bell shows an unread count.

New Entry

The prospect capture form. Key fields:

FieldNotes
Prospect nameSearchable dropdown backed by the Egyptian companies directory (live OpenCorporates reconciliation with a curated fallback). If the company is not listed, choose “Not included” and type the name — the Head of Products validates it.
Industry / SectorDropdown from the managed industries list.
Products of interestMulti-select from the managed product catalogue (tick all that apply).
Commercial Register no.Auto-filled when picked from the directory; editable.
Contact person / mobile / emailThe prospect's point of contact.
Company, responsible person, value, visit & close dates, attendees, summaryStandard pipeline fields. Attendees can be added from anywhere in the group.

On save: the leadership list is notified, the Administrator is alerted by email, the entry is set to Pending HoP — Validation, and you are taken to the Product Catalogue to consider cross-sell.

Pipeline & Pipeline Entry

The list of all pipeline entries and the detail page for each. The detail page shows prospect & contact details, products, lifecycle, attachments and the full comments / validation history, plus the action buttons available to you (status change, HoP review, manage entry, resubmit).

Master Ledger (All Merchants) & Client profile

All converted merchants across the group, searchable by name/status/company — check here before approaching a prospect to avoid duplicate outreach. From another company's client you can send an alignment request to its RM.

Good to Go list

Stagnant merchants and inactive pipeline entries released for group-wide re-engagement, newest activity first.

Companies & Product Catalogue

Reference for the group companies and the full product list (with referral contacts). The catalogue is also where an RM lands after saving an entry.

Bundles

Currently empty by design. After saving an entry, cross-sell is driven from the Product Catalogue.

Admin & Lists

The control centre (Admin / Head of Products). Tabs: Industries, Products, Egypt company source, Notification recipients, Codes & access, and (Admin only) Admin email log. Add/delete entries here and every dropdown across the platform updates immediately.

6. Step-by-step journeys by role

Employee / RM — submit a new prospect

  1. Sign in → click New Entry (sidebar) or + New pipeline entry.
  2. In Prospect name, start typing and pick the company from the directory. If it isn't there, click “Not included” and type the legal name.
  3. Select the industry, tick the products of interest, and enter Commercial Register no. and the contact person, mobile, email.
  4. Set the company, value, visit & close dates, attendees and a short summary.
  5. Click Save & notify leadership. You're taken to the Product Catalogue; the entry is now pending validation.
  6. Watch your Notifications: if the Head of Products returns it, open the entry, address the comment, and click Resubmit for validation.

Head of Products — validate & govern

  1. Open a Pending HoP — Validation entry from the dashboard or Notifications.
  2. Review the whole case (prospect, directory flag, products, contact, value).
  3. Choose one: Approve (→ First Meeting); Return — “please choose prospect name” (when a “Not included” prospect is actually in the directory); or Return to RM with a required comment.
  4. For extensions / done deals / edits / deletions / late entries, use the matching Approve / Reject buttons.
  5. Use Admin & Lists to keep the industries, products and Egypt source lists current.

CEO / MD — oversight

  1. Review the group-wide dashboard (exposure, active pipeline, Good to Go, alerts).
  2. Open any pipeline entry; if relevant, flag interest to join the client visit — the RM is notified to coordinate.
  3. Use the Master Ledger to see all merchants across companies.

Administrator — configure & monitor

  1. Open Admin & Lists.
  2. Industries / Products / Egypt source tabs: add or delete items; dropdowns update instantly.
  3. Notification recipients: review the distribution list; add a recipient (this also creates a login).
  4. Admin email log: see every submitted request that triggered an admin alert.
  5. Codes & access: merchant code scheme and the login/credential reference.

7. Administration & configuration

Admin email alerts

Every submitted request (new entry, extension, done deal, edit, delete, resubmit, alignment) raises an alert to the Administrator: an in-app notification to doaa.orfy plus a logged email to doaa.orfy@contact.eg, visible under Admin & Lists → Admin email log.

To deliver these to a real mailbox, set the server environment variables SMTP_HOST, SMTP_PORT, SMTP_USER, SMTP_PASS (and optionally SMTP_FROM). No code change is needed — the platform sends real email automatically once they're present and keeps logging in-app either way.

Managed lists & the Egypt source

Industries, products and the Egypt company fallback list are edited in Admin & Lists and shared across all users. The prospect dropdown first queries the live OpenCorporates Egypt reconciliation API; if it is unavailable it uses the curated fallback list, so prospect search always works.

Accounts

All employees in the directory have login accounts and can insert entries. The Administrator can add a recipient/login from the Recipients tab; new accounts get the default password Contact@123 and should be changed on first use in a production deployment.
